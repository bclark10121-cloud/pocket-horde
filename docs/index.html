Pocket Horde

Hold the garden. One thumb, six tools

You move. The tools do the rest.

You are a garden gnome with a slingshot and a lawn full of trouble. Aphids, slugs, beetles, wasps and moles pour in over the fence in timed waves. Your tools fire on their own, so the whole fight is where you stand. Scoop up the seeds pests drop, grow stronger after each wave, then spend seeds in the Shed on tools and trinkets. Merge tools to make them stronger. Pick a 10 or 20 wave run, then keep going in Endless after a win. One HD garden, three difficulty levels and four gnomes, three of them to unlock.

What kind of game is this?

One thumb. You only move. Up to six tools fire on their own at the nearest pest.
Ten or twenty waves. Five kinds of garden pests, from slow slugs to digging moles and stinging wasps. A short run takes about ten minutes. Win and you may keep going in Endless.
Build your belt. Twelve tools that merge into stronger tiers, 24 trinkets, a stat pick each time you grow and four gnomes with their own strengths.
A real way to lose. Health at zero ends the run. Gentle, Standard or Thorny. Your records, stats and achievements stay.

Things to do

  • Hold the Back Garden through 10 or 20 waves of aphids, slugs, beetles, wasps and moles.
  • Collect seeds to grow. Each level gives you a choice of three stat boosts.
  • Fill your belt with up to six of twelve garden tools. Merge two of the same into a stronger one.
  • Try the four new tools: plant a Sprinkler, send out bees, glow with the Firefly jar or push pests back with the Leaf blower.
  • Pick from 24 trinkets in the Shed. Reroll, lock and sell to shape your build.
  • Dodge beetle charges, wasp stingers and mole dirt. They all warn you in pink first.
  • Find the golden beetle hidden in the last wave.
  • Win a run, then keep going in Endless and set your deepest wave.
  • Unlock the Tinker, the Stout and the Hermit, then dress the Gardener in new hat and seed colors as your gnome level climbs.
  • Read up on every pest you send off in the Pest Almanac.
  • Finish three new daily goals each day for bonus seeds.
  • Earn three stars on each difficulty and run length, and 22 achievements.

Controls

Keyboard
WASD or arrows move the gnome. Esc opens the Menu. The mouse works the Shed.
Xbox controller
Left stick or D-pad moves the gnome. View or Menu opens the Menu. In the Shed and in menus A chooses and B goes back.
Touch
Put a thumb down anywhere and a stick appears under it. Slide to move. There is nothing else to press in a fight.

Play with an Xbox controller

Any Xbox Wireless Controller with Bluetooth works. That is every Series X|S controller and the Xbox One controllers made since 2016. If the plastic around the Xbox button matches the face of the controller, it has Bluetooth. Other controllers that your phone or browser sees as a standard gamepad use the same buttons.

On Android
  1. Hold the Xbox button to turn the controller on.
  2. Hold the small Pair button on top for 3 seconds, until the Xbox button flashes fast.
  3. On your phone, open Settings, then Connected devices, then Pair new device.
  4. Tap Xbox Wireless Controller. The Xbox button stays lit when it is connected.
  5. Open Pocket Horde. The touch buttons hide as soon as you use the controller.
In your browser
  1. Connect the controller to your computer with a USB cable or Bluetooth.
  2. Open Play in your browser. Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari all work.
  3. Press any button once after the game loads, so the browser sees the controller.
Menus
The D-pad or left stick moves between buttons. A chooses and B goes back. You can play the whole game with only the controller.

Install on Android

  1. Tap Download for Android on this page.
  2. Open the file. Allow installs from your browser if your phone asks.
  3. Tap Install, then Open.
  4. Later versions install over older ones and keep your records.

Android may show a Play Protect notice for apps from outside the Play Store. Version 0.4.0 installs over the last version and keeps your saves.
