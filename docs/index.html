Pocket Horde

Hold the garden. One thumb, six tools

You move. The tools do the rest.

You are a garden gnome with a slingshot and a lawn full of trouble. Aphids, slugs and beetles pour in over the fence in short timed waves. Your tools fire on their own, so the whole fight is where you stand. Scoop up the seeds the pests drop, then spend them in the Shed on more tools before the next wave. This first test build has one HD garden and five waves.

What kind of game is this?

One thumb. You only move. Up to six tools fire on their own at the nearest pest.
Short waves. Five timed waves in this build, 20 to 40 seconds each. A run takes about five minutes.
The Shed. Between waves, spend seeds on a Slingshot, a Trowel, a Watering can and four trinkets.
A real way to lose. Health at zero ends the run. Your records, stats and achievements stay.

Things to do

  • Hold the Back Garden through five waves of aphids, slugs and beetles.
  • Collect the seeds pests drop. Seeds left at the end of a wave count for half.
  • Fill your belt with up to six tools and watch them work together.
  • Dodge the beetle. It stops, flashes pink, then charges in a straight line.
  • Beat your best score. It saves on your device, in five save slots.
  • Earn ten achievements.
  • Turn on extra hearts, slower pests or softer hits in Settings if you want it easier.

Controls

Keyboard
WASD or arrows move the gnome. Esc opens the Menu. The mouse works the Shed.
Xbox controller
Left stick or D-pad moves the gnome. View or Menu opens the Menu. In the Shed and in menus A chooses and B goes back.
Touch
Put a thumb down anywhere and a stick appears under it. Slide to move. There is nothing else to press in a fight.

Play with an Xbox controller

Any Xbox Wireless Controller with Bluetooth works. That is every Series X|S controller and the Xbox One controllers made since 2016. If the plastic around the Xbox button matches the face of the controller, it has Bluetooth. Other controllers that your phone or browser sees as a standard gamepad use the same buttons.

On Android
  1. Hold the Xbox button to turn the controller on.
  2. Hold the small Pair button on top for 3 seconds, until the Xbox button flashes fast.
  3. On your phone, open Settings, then Connected devices, then Pair new device.
  4. Tap Xbox Wireless Controller. The Xbox button stays lit when it is connected.
  5. Open Pocket Horde. The touch buttons hide as soon as you use the controller.
In your browser
  1. Connect the controller to your computer with a USB cable or Bluetooth.
  2. Open Play in your browser. Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari all work.
  3. Press any button once after the game loads, so the browser sees the controller.
Menus
The D-pad or left stick moves between buttons. A chooses and B goes back. You can play the whole game with only the controller.

Install on Android

  1. Tap Download for Android on this page.
  2. Open the file. Allow installs from your browser if your phone asks.
  3. Tap Install, then Open.
  4. This is the first version, 0.1.0. Later versions install over it and keep your records.

Android may show a Play Protect notice for apps from outside the Play Store. Version 0.1.0 installs over the last version and keeps your saves.
