# Pocket Horde v0.1.0

The first test build. One level, the Back Garden, with the HD look: a painted garden, the gnome and three pests. Five waves to prove the swarm and the one thumb feel.

## Five minute test

1. Open the game and press Play, then a slot. You are in the garden within a few seconds. The gnome stands on the lawn with a slingshot.
2. Move with one thumb anywhere on the screen (or WASD, or the left stick). The slingshot fires on its own at the nearest pest.
3. Aphids come in clumps, slugs crawl in slowly. Walk over the yellow seeds they drop.
4. Wave 3 brings beetles. A beetle stops, shows a pink ring and a pink line, then charges along the line. Step off the line.
5. When the clock runs out, the Shed opens. Buy a Trowel or a Watering can, then press Next wave.
6. Turn the phone sideways and back mid-wave. The game keeps going and fills the screen.
7. Lose on purpose (stand still). The run end screen shows your wave, score and best. Press New run.

## New in this version

- The Back Garden: one HD painted garden seen from above, with beds, a pond, a stone path and a picket fence.
- HD toy render gnome, aphid, slug and beetle, with soft shadows. They face the way they walk.
- Five waves of 20 to 40 seconds. Three pest kinds. The beetle charges after a warning.
- Three tools that fire on their own: Slingshot, Trowel and Watering can (slows pests). Six tool slots.
- Seeds: pick them up as you move. Seeds left at the end of a wave fly to you at half value.
- The Shed between waves: four offers of tools and trinkets, bought with seeds.
- Health at zero ends the run. Records, stats and achievements stay. The run saves at every Shed visit, so Continue opens the Shed.
- Score and best score. Ten achievements and eight stats on the Records page.
- Easy switches in Settings: extra hearts, slower pests and softer hits.
- No boss battles (BC's call). One level only until the gameplay and graphics feel right.

5. Browser swarm test: `errors []`. A touch drag moves the gnome, the bot clears wave 1, the Shed opens, one buy works, Next wave starts wave 2. Pictures at 1280 x 720, 720 x 1280, 1920 x 1080 and 2400 x 1080.
