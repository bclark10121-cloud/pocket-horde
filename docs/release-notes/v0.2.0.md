# Pocket Horde v0.2.0

The full run. Ten waves in the Back Garden, two new pests, eight tools that merge, 24 trinkets, Grow picks, a full Shed and three difficulty levels. Still one level and no bosses.

## Five minute test

1. Start a new run. A small picker asks for Gentle, Standard or Thorny. Pick Standard.
2. Play wave 1. A thin bar under your health fills as you collect seeds. Level up and the Grow panel shows three stat picks after the wave. Pick one.
3. In the Shed: buy a tool, press Reroll (2 seeds, then it costs more), press Lock on an offer and check it is still there next visit.
4. Buy a second copy of the Slingshot, then press Merge. The belt shows Slingshot II.
5. Pick a tool on the belt and press Sell. You get half its price back.
6. Use Tester tools to jump to wave 6. Wasps hover at a distance and fire pink stingers. In wave 7, moles dig under the lawn as moving mounds, pop up after a pink ring and throw a dirt clod.
7. Turn the phone upright and open the Shed. It scrolls a little at the stats panel. Everything is readable.

## New in this version

- 10 waves, 20 to 75 seconds each. Waves 6 to 10 get tougher pests. Wave 10 is a long mixed swarm with a hidden golden beetle.
- New pests: the wasp (keeps its distance, fires stingers) and the mole (digs, pops up, throws dirt). Both shoot in pink with a dark outline.
- Eight tools: Slingshot, Trowel, Watering can, Rake, Hedge shears, Seed spitter, Garden hose and Bug zapper. All in HD toy art, circling the gnome.
- Merge two of the same tool into tier II, then tier III. Two or more tools of one kind give a set bonus.
- 24 trinkets with stat changes and eight special rules, such as a free first hit each wave or seeds that come in at full value.
- Grow: collect seeds to level up. Each level gives a pick of three stat boosts.
- The full Shed: rarity (common, uncommon, rare, shown by word and border shape), reroll, lock, sell, merge and a stats panel.
- Health no longer refills each wave. Regrowth gives some back. Armor cuts damage.
- Three difficulty levels: Gentle (softer pests, full heal each wave, cheaper Shed), Standard and Thorny. Best score kept for each.
- 15 achievements (5 new) and new stats: wins by difficulty, tools merged, most used tool.
- New HD art: wasp, mole, eight tools, golden seeds and leaf puffs.

## Judgment calls

- Balance: at the planned numbers the bot won every run, because the gnome's build grows much faster than the pests. Waves 6 to 10 now multiply pest health (2.2 times in wave 6 up to 7 times in wave 10) and damage (1.5 to 3.5 times). Tell me if late waves feel too hard or too soft.
- The rake, seed spitter and hose were redrawn in red, yellow and orange. Their first versions were green and vanished on the green screen.
- The difficulty name sits on the level line, because upright it ran into the health bar.
- Grow comes before the Shed. Continue reopens the same Grow picks.
- You cannot sell your last tool. Offers in the Shed are always tier I.
