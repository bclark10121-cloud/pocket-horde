# Pocket Horde v0.3.0

The look and the hook. A real start screen, music, sound, three gnomes, stars, daily goals and gnome level. Still one level and no bosses.

## Five minute test

1. Open the game. The start screen shows the gnome on a red toadstool at sunrise with the Pocket Horde title in a round red font. A wasp loops across the sky. The music plays on your first tap.
2. Press Play and a slot. Pick a difficulty. The picker now shows your stars on each one.
3. The gnome picker comes next. The Gardener is open. The Tinker and the Stout show how to unlock them.
4. Play a wave. Listen for soft pops, seed ticks and the wave start and clear jingles. The gameplay music differs from the start screen.
5. Open the Menu. Today's three daily goals show with progress. Open the Wardrobe and see the hat and seed colors you have.
6. Lose or win a run. The end screen shows your gnome level bar and the daily goals.
7. In Tester tools, use Unlock every gnome. Play a run as the Stout.

## New in this version

- The start screen: an HD painting of the gnome at the garden gate at sunrise, a looping wasp and drifting dandelion fluff. Title font Sniglet.
- Music: "Sneaky Snitch" on the start screen and "Monkeys Spinning Monkeys" in play. Both loop.
- Sound effects made for the game: pops, seed ticks, hits, slingshot, zap, spray, Shed chime, Grow chime, wave start and clear, run over and menu clicks.
- Three gnomes: the Gardener (balanced), the Tinker (faster tools, less health, unlocks after 10 merges) and the Stout (more health and armor, slower feet, unlocks by clearing wave 10).
- Stars: up to three per difficulty. Clear wave 5, clear wave 10, clear wave 10 with six tools.
- Daily goals: three a day from normal play. All three give 30 seeds at the start of your next run and a new seed color. No streaks.
- Gnome level: every run earns points, win or lose. Levels unlock hat colors for the Gardener and seed colors.
- The Wardrobe in the Menu picks your hat and seed colors.
- Completion percent in the Menu and on Records. 18 achievements (3 new).
- The fight line now says "Grow" for the in-run level, so it does not mix with gnome level.

## Shared kit

Built with kit 1.27.0. Godot 4.7.2.

## Install

Installs over 0.2.0 and 0.1.0. Your records stay. If you already cleared wave 10 or merged 10 tools, the Stout or the Tinker unlocks on its own.

## Judgment calls

- I picked the music, title font and start screen myself. Swap any of them.
- Gnome level 10 takes about two hours of play. Hats come at levels 2, 4, 6, 8 and 10, seed colors at 3, 5, 7 and 9.
- Stars show as words ("2 of 3 stars") because the game font has no star symbol.
- The Trowel, Rake and Shears make no sound of their own. The pest pop covers them.
- The gnome picker has a Back to levels button, so a wrong difficulty pick is not a dead end.
