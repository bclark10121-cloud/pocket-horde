# Pocket Horde v0.4.0

More to play. Pick a 10 or 20 wave run, keep going in Endless after a win, four new tools, a fourth gnome and the Pest Almanac. Still one level and no bosses.

## Five minute test

1. Open the game and press Play, then a slot. The difficulty picker now has a run length: 10 waves or 20 waves.
2. Pick 10 waves and Gentle. The gnome picker now shows four gnomes. The Hermit is locked and says how to unlock him.
3. Play two or three waves. In the Shed, look for the new tools: Sprinkler, Bee hive, Firefly jar and Leaf blower. Buy one and watch it work in the next wave.
4. Open the Menu. The Pest Almanac sits after the Wardrobe. Pests you have sent off show their picture and a short joke note.
5. In Tester tools, use Unlock every gnome. Start a new run as the Hermit. He has one tool slot and his tool hits three times as hard. His Shed offers Swap and Upgrade.
6. In Tester tools, jump to wave 10 and win the run. The end screen offers Keep going. Press it and Endless starts. Each Endless wave is harder. Your deepest wave is your record.
7. Send off all five regular pests in one run. The find star lights up even if the golden beetle got away.

## New in this version

- Run length: 10 waves (about 10 minutes) or 20 waves, picked on the difficulty picker. 20 wave runs keep their own stars and best scores.
- Endless: after a won run, Keep going plays on. Each Endless wave lasts 60 seconds, with pests 12 percent tougher and 6 percent more frequent than the one before. The win counts before Endless starts.
- Four new tools: Sprinkler (planted where you stand as each wave begins, slows pests around it), Bee hive (sends homing bees), Firefly jar (a glow that nips every pest close to you) and Leaf blower (a gust that pushes pests back). Twelve tools in all.
- The Hermit, a fourth gnome: one tool slot, triple damage, 10 percent faster tools, 2 more health. Unlock by clearing wave 10 with one tool on the belt, or at gnome level 5.
- The Pest Almanac in the Menu: every pest you have sent off, with a picture and a note.
- New stars, three per difficulty and run length: finish the run, send off all five regular pest kinds in one run and reach a score.
- Score stars: 10 waves Gentle 48,000, Standard 29,000, Thorny 50,000. 20 waves Gentle 96,000, Standard 100,000, Thorny 100,000.
- The golden beetle is a bonus, not part of the find star. It still hides in the last wave, with its own achievement and Almanac page.
- 22 achievements (4 new): Lone Gnome, Long Haul, Deep Roots and Almanac.
